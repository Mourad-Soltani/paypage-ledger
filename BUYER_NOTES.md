# Buyer notes — Paypage Ledger
Author: Mourad.Soltani

## Who buys this

Small Shopify / Woo / custom-checkout merchants and agencies who need a
simple script inventory for PCI DSS 4.x 6.4.3 conversations — not a
full enterprise page-integrity suite.

## Wedge

- Weekend-sized MVP
- Fixture-first so demos never depend on a live store
- Clear next paid features: live fetch + weekly email + SRI suggestion

## Pricing sketch

- $19 / store / month for weekly scans + email diff
- $49 / agency seat for 10 stores

## Do not position as

Full PCI certification. This is an inventory + change ledger, not an auditor.
